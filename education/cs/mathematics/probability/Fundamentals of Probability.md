Q:

Alice & Bob roll an equal sided die, the person with the highest number wins, what is the probability that Alice wins?

A:

Pr(A) + Pr(B) + Pr(D) = 1 ->
2Pr(A) + Pr(D) = 1

Probability of a draw = 1/6

Pr(A) = 5/12

Notes:

Need to take into account the edge case of there being a draw.

Omega:

Refers to the sample space or probability space.
Elements of Omega are elementary outcomes. For the above example, this would be the pairs of scores, such as 1:1, 1:2 ... 6:6


Kolmogorov Axioms:

- Probability is never negative -> Pr(a) >= 0
- Probability that the whole Omega is 1 -> Pr(O) = 1
- Probability adds up
